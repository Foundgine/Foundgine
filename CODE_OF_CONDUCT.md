# Code of conduct

We want Foundgine to be a welcoming, professional project for everyone who takes part in it.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion on topic and technical where possible.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews or issues.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may restrict participation by anyone who breaks these rules. Report
a problem through GitHub's private reporting on this repository or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are
handled confidentially.

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place where someone
represents the project.
