One consistent way to execute across your systems.

CRM, ERP, APIs, databases and AI agents should not each grow their own version of business meaning, validation and authorization. Foundgine is the configurable semantic and execution boundary between application capabilities and the interfaces that consume them.

Define meaning once.

Configure entities, fields, relationships and capabilities, then reuse them across application code, APIs, GraphQL and optional MCP callers.

Keep authority in the application.

Identity and policy stay with the host. Foundgine resolves and authorizes every operation before physical execution; an AI-generated request is never permission by itself.

Keep your existing stack.

Retain your domain applications, databases, EF Core or JPA, identity provider and workflow engine. Planning stays provider-independent until the execution boundary, so adoption can be incremental.

The business case is measurable: compare duplicated implementation, change effort, security-test coverage and operating cost before and after a representative pilot. Foundgine does not promise a universal percentage. Business and architecture case · Commercial strategy and measurement model

One lifecycle, many callers.

Application code, JSON, GraphQL, MCP and AI tools enter the same execution path. Retrieval can discover candidates, but it never becomes an authorization path.

  • Domain and semantic metadataWhat exists, what it means, and which capabilities are exposed.
  • AuthorizationDecided by the host’s identity and policy, before any plan.
  • Plan and ExecutionIRProvider-independent, with security provenance preserved through rewrites.
  • Providers and persistenceSQL, InMemory, EF Core and JPA/Hibernate do the physical work.

Open intent without open authority.

Foundgine gives AI agents a controlled capability surface without giving the model database authority. MCP is optional; the same boundary serves every other caller.

What the agent can do

  • Discover the capabilities the application exposes.
  • Express a request as structured intent.
  • Be asked to choose when candidates tie, instead of guessing.

What stays with the application

  • Authentication, actor identity and tenant context.
  • Authorization, re-evaluated for every actual operation.
  • Planning, validation and physical execution.
